01

Built for Kenya's Reality

SAP and Oracle weren't built for KRA eTIMS, NSSF Phase 3, SHA (formerly NHIF), or M-Pesa reconciliation. Our systems handle all Kenyan statutory requirements out of the box — no expensive add-ons, no workarounds. As of January 2026, any expense not backed by an eTIMS invoice is disallowed by KRA. Your system must comply. Ours does.

Support That Responds in Minutes

Failing to respond to a critical system issue within 5 minutes can paralyse your operations. Foreign providers run on US or European time zones — your emergency is their morning email. We are Nairobi-based, WhatsApp-reachable, and available 6 days a week. Your staff speak to real people who understand your exact setup, every time.

03

M-Pesa Native — Not an Afterthought

3.1 million businesses now accept M-Pesa payments — yet most accounting systems can't automatically reconcile them. Our systems integrate Lipa na M-Pesa, Paybill, Till Number, and Pochi la Biashara natively — every transaction automatically matched to the right invoice, customer, and account. No manual matching. No errors.

Lipa na M-PesaAuto-ReconciliationReal-Time Sync

Priced in KES. No Dollar Surprises.

When the KES depreciates against the dollar — which happens — your QuickBooks or Xero subscription quietly gets more expensive. Our pricing is fixed in Kenyan Shillings, transparent, and tiered for SMEs, meaning you pay exactly what you agreed and nothing more. No exchange rate exposure. No annual fee shock.

KES PricingNo Hidden FeesSME Tiers

Customised to Your Workflow

Off-the-shelf foreign software is built for a generic business in a generic country. Your school, hospital, SACCO, or retail chain has specific workflows, specific approval chains, and specific reports your board demands. We configure, customise, and train your team on a system that works the way you work — not the way Silicon Valley thinks you should.

Secure, Compliant & Audit-Ready
