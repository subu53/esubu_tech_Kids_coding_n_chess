Is it worth it?

KES 12,000 a month, next to what else is on that page

Nairobi has cheaper options, and some of them are good. So the useful question is not the monthly price — it is what a month of it actually buys your child.

Divide any monthly fee by the number of hours your child spends with a teacher, and then by the number of children sharing that teacher. That number — minutes of a teacher's attention per month — is the only one that predicts whether your child finishes something.

What twelve classes at Esubu are

  • About eighteen hours of live teaching in a month.
  • Capped at 15 children; one-to-one while numbers are small.
  • The same teacher from the first class to the certificate.
  • A finished project at the end, not a folder of exercises.

What a cheaper month often is

  • One session a week, so progress depends on your child practising alone in between.
  • A larger room, which is fine for a confident child and hard for a quiet one.
  • Often genuinely good value — for a child who is already self-driven.

If your child is the kind who finishes things on their own, a cheaper weekly club may well be the better buy, and I will tell you so on WhatsApp. This fee is for the child who needs someone watching closely enough to notice the week they stop trying.

Questions parents ask

Fees, payment and the awkward questions

How much do coding and chess classes cost at Esubu Academy?

KES 12,000 a month. Term classes run three evenings a week at 1.5 hours each, which works out at about KES 1,000 a class. Holiday camps run Monday to Friday, 9am to 12pm, for the same monthly fee. Payment is by M-Pesa once you accept a place.

Is there a registration fee?

No. The monthly fee is the only cost for the classes described on this page. You provide your child's device and internet connection; everything else the course needs runs on free software.

How many children will my child learn with?

Classes are capped at 15 children. While numbers are still small, every session is one-to-one with the founder, which is how we find out what each child actually understands. Group sessions begin as we add teachers, and families already enrolled choose their slot first.

Can I pay week by week instead of monthly?

Ask on WhatsApp. Not everyone can commit to a month up front, so week-by-week payment is possible on request. Sibling pricing is also available on request.

What happens if my child misses a class?

Tell me on WhatsApp as soon as you know. Sessions are live and not recorded, so a missed class is usually picked up in the next one, or in a short catch-up at the start. If the group's level turns out to be wrong in the first two or three weeks, your child moves to a group at the right level.

What does the fee include?

The same teacher throughout, written progress notes you can read at home, one finished project your child designed and built, and a certificate at the end of the camp or term.

Next step

Ask me the fee question directly

One WhatsApp message and you will get the fee, the timetable and the start date — no call centre, no sales call. If your child is not ready yet, I will say so.

The week-three worry. Most parents' real fear is not the price. It is paying for a month and watching their child lose interest in week three. That is a fair fear. If the level is wrong, your child moves to another group within the first three weeks. If your child is not ready at all, I will tell you before you pay anything.
