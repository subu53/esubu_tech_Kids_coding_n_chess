Who We Are

Not a Startup.
A Mission.

Kenya has 7.4 million SMEs. Fewer than 30% have a professional digital presence. Millions of children are leaving school without the technical skills the economy will demand. Most organisations are making critical decisions without data.

"We didn't build Esubu to chase a trend. We built it because every gap we serve is a gap we've seen firsthand — in Kenya's classrooms, in its markets, and in its businesses."

Every wing of Esubu is a direct response to a specific, observable problem in Kenya's digital ecosystem. That's what makes this different from a consultancy with a pitch deck. We've built communities, run businesses, and studied the data. Esubu is the product of that experience.

Sammy Subu

Founder, Esubu Tech · BSc Data Science

Online since 2008, Sammy co-founded Soko Huru — a marketplace that connected over 2,500 Kenyan SMEs to online markets and built a social community of 1.3 million. Frustrated by his own years of trying to learn technology without formal grounding, he returned to university in his thirties and earned a BSc in Data Science. The lessons from that journey — what works, what doesn't, and what Kenya's businesses and children actually need — became the foundation of Esubu.
